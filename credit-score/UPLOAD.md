## Description

There's a three-digit number that decides what you pay for a house, a car, an apartment, and sometimes whether you get the job. You never signed up for it, and three private companies build it about you every day.

This is the real story of the credit score:

- how two men turned people into math;
- what actually goes into your number;
- who makes billions from it;
- what it costs you;
- what you can do about it.
