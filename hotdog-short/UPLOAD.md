## Description
Costco has sold the same hot dog and soda for $1.50 since 1984. When the CEO asked to raise it in 2013, co-founder Jim Sinegal said: "If you raise the effing hot dog, I will kill you."

So how does a store that barely profits on its food court make billions? In fiscal 2025, Costco made $10.4 billion in operating income. $5.3 billion came from membership fees, and about nine in ten members renew.
