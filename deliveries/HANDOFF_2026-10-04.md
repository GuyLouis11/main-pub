Gas Stations Make 13 Cents a Gallon. Here's the Real Money.

DESCRIPTION:
Every time you fill up, the gas station keeps about 13 cents a gallon after costs. Card fees alone cost the station more than 8 cents a gallon.

So why sell gas at all? Because gas is the bait. Fuel is 65% of what convenience stores sell, but only about 39% of their gross profit. The rest comes from inside: coffee, hot dogs, energy drinks and snacks. And 57% of drivers who fill up walk in.

McDonald's Makes $10.4 Billion From Rent, Not Burgers

DESCRIPTION:
McDonald's biggest business isn't food. In 2025 it collected $10.4 billion in rent from franchisees, versus about $6 billion in royalties on their sales.

About 95% of McDonald's restaurants are franchised, and in its main markets McDonald's owns the land under about 56% of them and roughly 80% of the buildings. As its 1950s finance chief Harry Sonneborn is often quoted: "We are not technically in the food business. We are in the real estate business."
