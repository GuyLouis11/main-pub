## Description
Eighty feet under Liberty Street in Manhattan sits the largest gold vault in the world: about 507,000 bars and 6,331 metric tons, more than Fort Knox. Almost none of it belongs to the United States.

The strangest part: when one country pays another in gold, nothing gets shipped. A vault worker wheels the bars from one locked cage to the cage next door.
