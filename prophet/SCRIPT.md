# THE PROPHET — script

*A fictional story built on a real scam.* Twist 1: the 10,240-person halving. Twist 2: the narrator sent the emails.


## COLD OPEN

**P01** · *alarm* — Every Monday morning, at exactly six a.m., Daniel Hale got an email from someone he'd never met.
**P02** · *sentence* — It was always one sentence. What the stock market would do that week.
**P03** · *sentence* — Up. Down. Down. Up.
**P04** · *never* — The sender was never wrong. Not once. Ten weeks in a row.
**P05** · *never* — The odds of guessing that are one in one thousand and twenty-four.
**P06** · *offer0* — On the eleventh Monday, the email asked for fifty thousand dollars.
**P07** · *stakes* — Daniel had a wife, a mortgage, and a daughter about to start college. And ten perfect predictions sitting in his inbox.
**P08** · *nobody* — Nobody knows who sent those emails. But by the end of this, you'll know exactly how it worked.

**P17** · *w2w3* — Week three. Down. It went down.

## 10,240

**P68** · *ten* — After week eight: forty. Week nine: twenty. Week ten…
**P69** · *ten* — Ten people. Ten people who had watched someone predict the market ten times in a row. Perfectly.
**P70** · *survived* — Daniel wasn't chosen. He survived. Like a coin that lands heads ten times in a row, because ten thousand coins were flipped.
