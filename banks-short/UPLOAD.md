## Description
When a bank gives you a loan, it doesn't hand you someone else's savings. It types the number into your account, and new money exists.

The "deposit $10,000, the bank keeps 10%, lends out the rest" story is the version most people learn. The Bank of England calls it a misconception. In the U.S., the reserve requirement has been 0% since March 2020. And when you pay the loan back, that money is destroyed.

Educational content, not financial advice.

Sources:
