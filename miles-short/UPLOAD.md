## Description
In 2025, American Express paid Delta $8.2 billion, mostly to buy SkyMiles for its cardholders. Delta's entire pre-tax profit that year was $6.2 billion.

It's not just Delta. In 2020, United borrowed against its MileagePlus program, valued at $21.9 billion, about twice the airline's equity value at the time.
